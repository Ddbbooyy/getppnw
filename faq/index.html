What is fulvic acid and what does it do?
Fulvic acid is a naturally occurring low-molecular-weight organic compound formed from ancient plant decomposition. It acts as a cellular delivery system — chelating minerals into bioavailable forms small enough to cross cell membranes, increasing nutrient absorption by up to 130%. Think of it as the "transport layer" your body is missing because industrial agriculture destroyed the soil microbiome that used to produce it naturally.
What is humic acid and how is it different from fulvic acid?
Humic acid is a high-molecular-weight compound that acts as a toxin binder. While fulvic acid delivers nutrients INTO cells (Road IN), humic acid binds heavy metals, pesticides, and environmental toxins and escorts them OUT of the body (Road OUT). They complement each other but must be taken separately — fulvic in the AM, humic in the PM — because a binder and a transporter running simultaneously compete.

When should I take fulvic acid?
Morning, on an empty stomach, 20 minutes before food. Mix one sachet in 8-12oz of water. This allows the transport layer to establish before you consume nutrients — making everything you eat afterward more bioavailable.
When should I take humic acid?
Evening, at least 2 hours after your last meal and away from any medications or other supplements. Humic acid is a binder — if you take it with food or medications, it may bind things you want to absorb. Separation is the key to effectiveness.
Can I take fulvic and humic acid at the same time?
No — and this is the biggest mistake most brands make by blending them into one product. Humic acid binds minerals. Fulvic acid delivers minerals. Running them simultaneously creates a molecular tug-of-war. Separate them by at least 8-10 hours for full effectiveness.
