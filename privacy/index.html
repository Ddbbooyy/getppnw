Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Pure Path Northwest LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our websites, applications, and services.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Account information: name, email address, age, biological sex
  • Health profile data: health goals, current supplements, medications (Path AI advisor)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store card numbers)
  • Order information: shipping address, phone number
  • Communications: emails, support requests, reviews, feedback
  • Newsletter subscription: email address, first name

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Device and browser information
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, session duration
  • Log data: IP address, access times, referring URL

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process transactions and fulfill orders
  • Personalize AI-generated health education content (Path)
  • Send transactional communications (order confirmations, shipping updates)
  • Send periodic newsletter emails (opt-out available via unsubscribe link in every email)
  • Respond to your requests and questions
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored on secure infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare D1 database, GitHub encrypted repositories). We implement HTTPS encryption, access controls, and regular security practices. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: Cloudflare (hosting), Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Google (AI via Gemini API) — strictly for service operation
  • Legal compliance: when required by law, subpoena, or court order
  • Business transfer: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale
  • Protection: to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public

5. AI-Specific Data Practices

When you use Path (AI Health Advisor):

  • Conversation history is stored to provide personalized recommendations
  • Health profile data is sent to Google Gemini API for response generation
  • Google's data retention and privacy policies apply to API interactions
  • You may request deletion of your conversation history at any time
  • We do not use your health data for advertising purposes

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Access: request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out: unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
  • Portability: request data in machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact dannyppnw@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. HIPAA Notice

Pure Path Northwest LLC is NOT a HIPAA-covered entity. We are not a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse. Information shared with Path is not Protected Health Information (PHI) under HIPAA.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it promptly.

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for site functionality. We use Google Analytics 4 for aggregate usage analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Our newsletter uses standard email open/click tracking.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You may request deletion at any time.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be posted with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
