Questions

Straight Answers

Is this shilajit?
No. Shilajit is crude geological resin — only 15–20% fulvic acid, mixed with uncontrolled material and documented heavy metal contamination. This is isolated, cold-extracted fulvic and humic acid from controlled Utah deposits. Same active molecule. None of the geological lottery.
Why separate fulvic and humic instead of blending them?
Because a binder and a transporter running simultaneously compete. Humic can bind the same minerals fulvic is trying to deliver. We separate them in time: fulvic AM (delivery), humic PM (binding). Each molecule at full strength.
When do I take each sachet?
Fulvic: morning, empty stomach, 20 min before food. Humic: evening, 2+ hours after food, away from medications. The protocol card walks you through exact timing.
How do I know it's clean?
Every batch ships with a third-party Certificate of Analysis covering heavy metals and potency. Lot-tracked. Auditable. We source from ancient pre-industrial Utah deposits — a fundamentally different contamination risk profile than mountain resin.
